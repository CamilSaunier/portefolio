Obsidian GSync

Obsidian GSync is a synchronization solution that allows users to synchronize their Obsidian vault files across devices using their own Google Drive account.

How it works

Synchronization is handled by the GSync community plugin for Obsidian, which uses the Google Drive API to transfer files between the user's devices and their own Google Drive account.

Authentication

Google OAuth is used to authorize access to Google Drive. The authentication callback page redirects the one-time authorization response back to the Obsidian application.

Privacy

This website does not collect or store Obsidian vault contents or Google Drive files.
