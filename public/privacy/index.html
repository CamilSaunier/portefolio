Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Overview

Obsidian GSync is a synchronization setup that allows users to synchronize their Obsidian vault files through their own Google Drive account using the GSync community plugin.

Data Access and Use

The GSync plugin uses Google OAuth and the Google Drive API to access the user's Google Drive account for synchronization purposes. Users choose to authorize this access themselves.

Vault files synchronized through the plugin are stored in the user's own Google Drive account. This portfolio website does not collect, access, or store those vault files.

Authentication

The authentication callback page hosted on this domain passes the one-time authorization response from Google back to the Obsidian application. It does not operate a server-side authentication service or store the authorization code.

Data Sharing

This website does not sell or share personal information. Google Drive access is governed by Google's privacy policy and the permissions granted by the user.

Data Retention and Revocation

Users can revoke the application's Google account access at any time through their Google Account security settings.
